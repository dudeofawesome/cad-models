# Physical

## Material

- aluminum bar / angle with rivets is a starting suggestion, not a requirement; other construction (e.g. T-slot extrusion, off-the-shelf rack shelves, bolts) is acceptable if it fits the budget
- tool availability does not constrain the design unless a tool is expensive to buy or rent
- an open skeleton frame (no outer walls) is being considered; any walls or ducts it needs for airflow should be easy to remove for service

## Rack

- APC (Schneider Electric) cabinet, standard EIA-310 19" with square holes
- rail hole spacing side to side: 18.25" (465 mm) center to center
- front-to-rear rail spacing: 36.25" (921 mm), adjustable shorter in 1" steps
- the cabinet is otherwise nearly empty; chassis height is flexible

## Mounting

- fixed mounting (shelf or fixed rails) is acceptable; slide rails are optional
- mounting must carry the full machine, roughly 30 kg or more

## Size

- 4U is a suggestion, not a constraint
- allow space for component cooling, connected cables, and service access when choosing chassis dimensions
