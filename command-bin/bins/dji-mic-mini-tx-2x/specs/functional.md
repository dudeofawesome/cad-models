# Functional

## Usability

- must house 2 DJI Mic Mini TX charging docks with both powered via USB-C
- must be able to remove TX units from charging docs by lifting unit upwards
- TX units have a strong magnet on the back of them (for clipping through shirts). Care must be taken for 2 units' magnets to not align with each other.

## Compatibility

- should include friction-fit USB cable-routing channels along the top front and top back for the cables from other bins. consider a nominal cable diameter to be 3.2mm. make the channels deep enough to hold at least 2 cables.
